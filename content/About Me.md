# Bio
Originally from just outside of Philadelphia, I spent some time living in Madison, Wisconsin before moving to Portland, Maine, where I currently reside. I graduated from Vassar College in June of 2021, majoring in cognitive science with a minor in sociology. In addition to my primary studies, I took courses in art history, urban studies, philosophy, media studies, and computer science to name a few. I work as a Product Designer at [IntelAgree](https://www.intelagree.com/) and recently completed my Masters in Data Science at Northeastern's Roux Institute.
